Chronic absenteeism in DC public schools, 2024–25

Analysis
Question
Which DC students and schools are most affected by chronic absenteeism, and which schools do better than expected for the students they serve?
What I did
Cleaned and analyzed OSSE's public school report card data for 229 schools in Python. Compared each school with its expected absenteeism rate, based on its share of economically disadvantaged students.
Findings
40% of DC students were chronically absent, rising from 34% in middle school to 55% in high school. Five high-poverty schools had rates 22 to 37 points below expected, which makes them models for other schools to learn from.

Data-informed intervention for a 6th-grade class

Case study
Problem
Students had wide gaps in literacy and numeracy, and there was no simple way to see who needed what support.
What I did
Built progress dashboards in Google Sheets, ran root-cause analysis on attendance and assessment data, and designed targeted interventions for each group of students.
Result
Overall academic performance rose 25% over the year, and low-performing students improved 40%. I presented the results at the TFI Showcase.
